**Intro. to Sociology** `SOC 101`
- *You May Ask Yourself: An Intro. to Thinking Like a Sociologist* by Dalton Conley

**Gender, Sexuality & Migration** `SOC 293`
- *Sex Trafficking: Inside the Business of Modern Slavery* by Siddharth Kara
- *Intimate Migrations: Gender, Family & Illegality among Transnational Mexicans* by Deborah A. Boehm
- *High-Tech Housewives: Indian IT Workers, Gendered Labor & Transmigration* by Amy Bhatt
- *The Trafficker Next Door: How Household Employers Exploit Domestic Workers* by Rhacel Salazar Parreñas
- *The Qualities of a Citizen: Women, Immigration & Citizenship, 1870-1965* by Martha Gardner

**Musical Theatre & Fan Cultures** `SOC 376`
- *Fandom in Musical Theatre* by Jessica Hillman-McCord
- *John Proctor is the Villain* by Kimberly Belflower

**Gender in a Global Society** `SOC 507`
- *Caliban & the Witch: Women, the Body & Primitive Accumulation* by Silvia Federici
- *The Indebted Woman: Kinship, Sexuality & Capitalism* by Isabelle Guérin
- *Patriarchy & Accumulation on a World Scale: Women in the International Division of Labour* by Maria Mies
- *Politics of Piety: The Islamic Revival and the Feminist Subject* by Saba Mahmood
- *Decolonizing Extinction: The Work of Care in Orangutan Rehabilitation* by Juno Salazar Parreñas
- *Illicit Flirtations: Labor, Migration & Sex Trafficking in Tokyo* by Rhacel Salazar Parreñas
- *Do Muslim Women Need Saving?* by Lila Abu-Lughod
