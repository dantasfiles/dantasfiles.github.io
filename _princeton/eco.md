**Intro. to Macroeconomics** `ECO 101`
- *Macroeconomics: Principles & Policy* by Baumol et al.

**Macroeconomics** `ECO 301`
- *Macroeconomics* by Abel et al.

**Econometrics** `ECO 302`
- *Intro. to Econometrics* by Stock & Watson 

**Microeconomic Theory: A Mathematical Approach** `ECO 310`
- *A Short Course in Intermediate Microeconomics w/ Calculus* by Serrano & Feldman

**Environmental Economics** `ECO 329`
- *Economics and the Environment* by Goodstein & Polasky

**Microeconomic Theory I** `ECO 501`
- *Microeconomic Foundations II: Imperfect Competition, Information & Strategic Interaction* by David M. Kreps
- *An Intro. to Game Theory* by Martin J. Osborne

**Macroeconomic Theory I** `ECO 503`
- *Recursive Methods in Economic Dynamics* by Stokey et al.

**Econometric Theory I** `ECO 517`
- *Econometrics* by Fumio Hayashi
- *Econometrics* by Bruce Hansen 

**Asset Pricing** `ECO 525`
- *Dynamic Asset Pricing Theory* by Darrell Duffie 

**International Trade I** `ECO 551`
- *Theory of International Trade: A Dual, General Equilibrium Approach* by Dixit & Norman
- *Advanced International Trade: Theory & Evidence* by Robert C. Feenstra
- *Market Structure & Foreign Trade: Increasing Returns, Imperfect Competition & the International Economy* by Helpman & Krugman
