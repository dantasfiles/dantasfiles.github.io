---
title: 🐯 Princeton Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering unofficial reading list, fall 2026
---

**Engineering in the Modern World** `MAE 102`
- *The Innovators, Trade: The Engineering Pioneers Who Transformed America* by David P. Billington
- *From Insight to Innovation: Engineering Ideas That Transformed America in the 20th Century* by Billington & Littman
- *Power, Speed & Form: Engineers & the Making of the 20th Century* by Billington & Billington 

**Thermodynamics** `MAE 221`
- *Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach* by Cengel et al.

**Structure & Properties of Materials** `MAE 324`
- *Materials Science & Engineering: An Intro.* by Callister & Rethwisch

**Matrix Structural Analysis & Intro. to Finite-Element Methods** `MAE 325`
- *An Intro. To Matrix Structural Analysis & Finite Element Methods* by Prevost & Bagrianski

**Origami Engineering** `MAE 327 / 556`
- *How to Fold It: The Mathematics of Linkages, Origami & Polyhedra* by Joseph O'Rourke 

**Aircraft Flight Dynamics** `MAE 331`
- *Flight Dynamics* by Robert F. Stengel

**[Intro. to Robotics](https://irom-lab.princeton.edu/intro-to-robotics/)** `MAE 345`
- *[Principles of Robot Autonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03496)* by Gammelli et al.
- *[Planning Algorithms](https://lavalle.pl/planning/)* by Steven M. LaValle
- *Probabilistic Robotics* by Thrun et al.

**Modern Control** `MAE 434`
- *Linear Systems Theory* by João P. Hespanha 

**Mathematical Methods of Engineering Analysis I** `MAE 501`
- *Mathematical Methods for Physics & Engineering* by Riley et al.
- *Mathematical Methods for Physicists* by Arfken et al.
