## Sophomore level

**Introduction to Cellular & Molecular Biology** `CBE 214`
- *Essential Cell Biology* by Alberts et al. 

## Graduate level

**Mathematical Methods of Engineering Analysis I** `CBE 509`
- *Mathematical Methods for Physics & Engineering* by Riley et al.
- *Mathematical Methods for Physicists* by Arfken et al.

**Membrane Separations for Energy & the Environment** `CBE 513`
- *Membrane Technology & Applications* by Richard W. Baker

**Intro. to Statistical Mechanics** `CBE 524`
- *Intro. to Modern Statistical Mechanics* by David Chandler
- *Random Heterogeneous Materials: Microstructure & Macroscopic Properties* by Salvatore Torquato

**Technical Leadership in Engineering** `CBE 531`
- *The 8 Dimensions of Leadership: DiSC Strategies for Becoming a Better Leader* by Sugerman et al.
- *Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance* by Angela Duckworth
- ✔️ ***Designing Your Life: How to Build a Well-Lived, Joyful Life*** by Burnett & Evans ([my highlights](https://www.goodreads.com/notes/62036224-designing-your-life/185327722-daniel-dantas))
- *Managing Scientists: Leadership Strategies in Scientific Research* by Alice M. Sapienza
- *Leadership by Engineers & Scientists: Professional Skills Needed to Succeed in a Changing World* by Dennis W. Hess
