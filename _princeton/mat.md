**Calculus I** / **II** / **One Variable Calculus w/ Proofs** `MAT 103 / 104 / 210`
- *Thomas' Calculus, Early Transcendentals: Single Variable* by Hass et al.

**Math. for Economics / Life Sciences** `MAT 175`
- *Calculus* by Varberg et al.
- *Thomas' Calculus, Multivariable* by Hass et al.

**Multivariable Calculus** `MAT 201`
- *Thomas' Calculus, Multivariable* by Hass et al. 

**Linear Algebra w/ Applications** `MAT 202`
- *Linear Algebra w/ Applications* by Otto Bretscher

**Adv. Vector Calculus** `MAT 203`
- *Vector Calculus* by Marsden & Tromba

**Numbers, Equations & Proofs** `MAT 214`
- *The Higher Arithmetic: An Intro. to the Theory of Numbers* by H. Davenport
- *A Classical Intro. to Modern Number Theory* by Ireland & Rosen
- *An Intro. to the Theory of Numbers* by Ivan Niven

**Single Variable Analysis w/ an Intro. to Proofs** `MAT 215`
- *Understanding Analysis* by Stephen Abbott

**Multivariable Analysis & Linear Algebra I** `MAT 216`
- *An Intro. to Analysis* by Robert C. Gunning 

**Multivariable Analysis I** `MAT 300`
- *Mathematical Analysis I* by Vladimir A. Zorich

**Intro. to Real Analysis** `MAT 320`
- *Real Analysis* by Royden & Fitzpatrick 

**Numerical Analysis & Scientific Computing** `MAT 321`
- *Numerical Linear Algebra* by Trefethen and Bau
- *An Introduction to Numerical Analysis* by Süli & Mayers

**Analysis II: Complex Analysis** `MAT 335`
- *Complex Analysis* by Stein & Shakarchi

**Applied Algebra** `MAT 340`
- *Groups and Symmetry* by Mark A. Armstrong 

**Algebra I** `MAT 345`
- *Algebra* by Michael Artin 

**Topology** `MAT 365`
- *Topology* by James Munkres

**Probability & Stochastic Systems** `MAT 380`
- *Fundamentals of Probability: With Stochastic Processes* by Saeed Ghahramani
- *Intro. To Probability* by Bertsekas & Tsitsiklis
- *Intro. to Probability Models* by Sheldon M. Ross

**Geometry & Number Theory: Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves** `MAT 419`
- *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves* by Joseph H. Silverman

**Algebraic Geometry** `MAT 457`
- *Basic Algebraic Geometry 1: Varieties in Projective Space* by Shafarevich & Reid
