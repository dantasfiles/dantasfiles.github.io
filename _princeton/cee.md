title: 🐯 Princeton Civil & Environmental Engineering unofficial reading list, fall 2026

**Engineering in the Modern World** `CEE 102`
- *The Innovators, Trade: The Engineering Pioneers Who Transformed America* by David P. Billington
- *From Insight to Innovation: Engineering Ideas That Transformed America in the 20th Century* by Billington & Littman
- *Power, Speed & Form: Engineers & the Making of the 20th Century* by Billington & Billington

**Mechanics of Solids** `CEE 205`
- *Mechanics of Materials* by R.C. Hibbeler

**Applied Statistical Methods in the Natural & Built Environment** `CEE 323/523`
- *Applied Statistics for CEE* by Kottegoda & Rosso

**Origami Engineering** `CEE 345/545`
- *How to Fold It: The Mathematics of Linkages, Origami & Polyhedra* by Joseph O'Rourke

**Matrix Structural Analysis & Intro. to Finite-Element Methods** `CEE 361`
- *An Intro. To Matrix Structural Analysis & Finite Element Methods* by Prevost & Bagrianski

**Design of Reinforced Concrete Structures** `CEE 366`
- *Design of Concrete Structures* by Darwin & Dolan

**Autonomous Fabrication & Robotics** `CEE 374`
- *3D Printing of Concrete: Properties, Materials & Modeling* by Zhanzhao Li and Aleksandra Radlińska
- *Robotics: Theory & Industrial Applications* by Ross et al.

**Risk Analysis** `CEE 460`
- *Probability Concepts in Engineering: Emphasis on Applications to CEE* by Ang & Tang
- *An Intro. to Statistical Modeling of Extreme Values* by Stuart Coles

**Intro. to Water Pollution Technology** `CEE 471`
- *Water Quality: Characteristics, Modeling & Modification* by Tchobanoglous & Schoeder 

**Membrane Separations for Energy & the Environment** `CEE 504`
- *Membrane Technology & Applications* by Richard W. Baker

**Holistic Analysis of Heritage Structures** `CEE 538`
- *Concrete Vaulted Construction in Imperial Rome: Innovations in Context* by Lynne C. Lancaster
- *Innovative Vaulting in the Architecture of the Roman Empire: 1st to 4th Centuries CE* by Lynne C. Lancaster
- *Building Construction Before Mechanization* by John Fitchen
- *The Oxford Handbook of Engineering & Technology in the Classical World* by John Peter Oleson

**Ecohydrology** `CEE 587`
- *Ecohydrology: Dynamics of Life & Water in the Critical Zone* by Porporato & Yin 

**Modeling Environmental Geochemistry** `CEE 599`
- *Aqueous Environmental Geochemistry* by Donald Langmuir
- *Description of Input & Examples for PHREEQC Version 3: A Computer Program for Speciation, Batch-Reaction, One-Dimensional Transport & Inverse Geochemical Calculations* by Parkhurst & Apello
- *Kinetics of Water-Rock Interaction* by Brantley et al.
- *Environmental Modeling: Fate & Transport of Pollutants in Water, Air & Soil* by Jerald L. Schnoor
