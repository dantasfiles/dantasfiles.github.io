## Freshman level

**Writing Place & the Environment** `NTRES 1200-101`
- *A Sand County Almanac* by Aldo Leopold

## Sophomore level

**The Art of Writing Field Observation** `NTRES 2150`
- *The Lost Art of Reading Nature's Signs: Use Outdoor Clues to Find Your Way, Predict the Weather, Locate Water, Track Animals & Other Forgotten Skills* by Tristan Gooley
- *A Sand County Almanac: With Other Essays on Conservation from Round River* by Aldo Leopold

## Junior level

**Intro. to Biogeochemistry** `NTRES 3030`
- *Principles of Terrestrial Ecosystem Ecology* by Chapin et al.
- *Environmental & Low-Temperature Geochemistry* by Peter Ryan

**Environmental Governance** `NTRES 3311/6310`
- *The Politics of the Earth* by John S. Dryzek

## Senior level

**Wildlife Corridor Conservation & Crossing Design** `NTRES 4800/5800`
- *Crossings: How Road Ecology Is Shaping the Future of Our Planet* by Ben Goldfarb
