**Continuing Spanish** `SPAN 1230 (fa26)`

**Narrating the Spanish Civil War** `SPAN 1305 (fa26)`
- *Requiem for a Spanish Peasant* by Ramón J. Sender

## Sophomore level

**Spanish Intermediate Composition & Conversation II** `SPAN 2095 (fa26)`

- *Manual de gramática* by Dozier & Iguina

## Junior level

**Global Latin America** `SPAN 3440 (fa26)`
- *Fictions* by Jorge Luis Borges
- *Global Latin America: Into the 21st Century* by Lesser & Gutmann
- *Nazi Literature in the Americas* by Roberto Bolaño
- *The Dangers of Smoking in Bed* by Mariana Enriquez

**Diasporas, Disasters & Dissent: Re-Thinking Puerto Rican Studies in the 20th & 21st Centuries** `SPAN 3675 (fa26)`
- *Bodega Dreams* by Ernesto Quiñonez
- *The Meaning of Consuelo* by Judith Ortiz Cofer 

## Senior level

**Moses Maimonides** `SPAN 4540 (fa26)`

- *A Maimonides Reader* by Isadore Twersky
- *Maimonides in His World: Portrait of a Mediterranean Thinker* by Sarah Stroumsa
- *Maimonides: The Life & World of One of Civilization's Greatest Minds* by Joel L. Kraemer
- *The Guide of the Perplexed* by Moses Maimonides
