title: 🐻 Cornell Africana Studies & Research Center unofficial reading list, fall 2026
---

## Freshman level

**Elementary Arabic I** `ASRC 1201 (fa26)`
- *Arabiyyat al-Naas, Part 1: An Intro. Course in Arabic* by Younes et al.  

**Intermediate Arabic I** `ASRC 1203 (fa26)`
- *Arabiyyat al-Naas, Part 2: An Intermediate Course in Arabic* by Younes et al. 

**African American History from 1865** `ASRC 1595 (fa26)`
- *Race, Reform, and Rebellion: The Second Reconstruction & Beyond in Black America, 1945-2006* by Manning Marable

**Recreating the Caribbean: Migration & Identity in Contemporary Caribbean History** `ASRC 1976 (fa26)`
- *A Concise History of the Caribbean* by B.W. Higman 

**American History from 1500 to 1800** `ASRC 1985 (fa26)`
- *American Colonies: The Settling of North America* by Alan Taylor 

## Sophomore level

**Arabic for Heritage Speakers** `ASRC 2105 (fa26)`
- *Riḥla ilā Bilād al-‘Arab: A Comprehensive Intro. Course for Arabic Heritage Speakers* by Munther Younes

**Histories of the African Diaspora** `ASRC 2317 (fa26)`
- *The Americans Are Coming!: Dreams of African American Liberation in Segregationist South Africa* by Robert Trent Vinson
- *Jah Kingdom: Rastafarians, Tanzania & Pan-Africanism in the Age of Decolonization* by Monique A. Bedasse
- *All That She Carried: The Journey of Ashley's Sack, a Black Family Keepsake* by Tiya Miles 

**The Novels of Toni Morrison** `ASRC 2603 (fa26)`
- *Paradise* by Toni Morrison

**The History & Politics of Modern Egypt** `ASRC 2670 (fa26)`
- *Ordinary Egyptians: Creating the Modern Nation through Popular Culture* by Ziad Fahmy
- *Modern Egypt: The Formation Of A Nation State* by Arthur Goldschmidt Jr.

## Junior level

**Zora Neale Hurston & Langston Hughes** `ASRC 3705 (fa26)`
- *Their Eyes Were Watching God* by Zora Neale Hurston
- *Tar Baby* by Toni Morrison
- *Mules & Men* by Zora Neale Hurston
- *The Collected Poems of Langston Hughes*
- *Mule Bone* by Hurston & Hughes
- *Barracoon: The Story of the Last "Black Cargo"* by Zora Neale Hurston
- *Recyclopedia* by Harryette Mullen

## Senior level

**Contesting Identities in Modern Egypt** `ASRC 4650 (fa26)`
- *Sacred Language, Ordinary People: Dilemmas of Culture & Politics in Egypt* by Niloofar Haeri
- *Ordinary Egyptians: Creating the Modern Nation through Popular Culture* by Ziad Fahmy
- *Karnak Café* by Naguib Mahfouz
- *A Border Passage: From Cairo to America -- A Woman's Journey* by Leila Ahmed
- *Industrial Sexuality: Gender, Urbanization & Social Transformation in Egypt* by Hanan Hammad
- *An Incurable Past: Nasser's Egypt Then and Now* by Mériam N. Belli
- *Egypt in the Future Tense: Hope, Frustration, and Ambivalence before & after 2011* by Samuli Schielke
- *Identifying with Nationality: Europeans, Ottomans & Egyptians in Alexandria* by Will Hanley
- *Nile Nightshade: An Egyptian Culinary History of the Tomato* by Anny Gaul
