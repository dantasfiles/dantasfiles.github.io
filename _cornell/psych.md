## Freshman level

**Intro. to Human Development** `PSYCH 1131`
- *Life-Span Development* by John W. Santrock

**Intro. to Environmental Psychology** `PSYCH 1500` / **IEP: Writing in the Major** `PSYCH 1501`
- *The Image of the City* by Kevin Lynch
- *The Hidden Dimension* by Edward T. Hall

## Sophomore level

**Intro. to Psychopathology** `PSYCH 2250`
- *Psychopathology: An Integrative Approach to Mental Disorders* by Barlow et al.

**Intro. to Community Psychology** `PSYCH 2400`
- *Community Psychology: In Pursuit of Liberation & Wellbeing* by Riemer et al.

**Intro. to Social Psychology** `PSYCH 2800`
- *Social Psychology* by Gilovich et al.

**Research Methods in Human Development** `PSYCH 2830`
- *A Short Guide to Writing about Psychology* by Dana S. Dunn
- *Research Methods in Psychology* by Beth Morling

## Junior level

**Memory & the Law** `PSYCH 3190`
- *Memory* by Baddeley et al.
- *The Science of False Memory* by Brainerd & Reyna
