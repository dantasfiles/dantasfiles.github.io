## Freshman level

**Culture, Society & Power: Person, Time & Conduct** `ANTHR 1101-101`
- *Stories as Equipment for Living: Last Talks and Tales of Barbara Myerhoff* 

## Sophomore level 

**The Natural History of Chimpanzees & the Origins of Politics** `ANTHR 2310`
- *In The Shadow Of Man* by Jane Goodall
- *Chimpanzee Politics* by Frans de Waal
- *Wild Chimpanzees: Social Behavior of an Endangered Species* by Adam Clark Arcadi
 
**Cultural Diversity & Contemporary Issues** `ANTHR 2400 (su26)`
- *The Land of Open Graves: Living and Dying on the Migrant Trail* by De Leon & Wells
- *Warning Signs of Genocide: An Anthropological Perspective* by Anderson & Anderson
 
**Nature-Culture: Ethnographic Approaches to Human Environment Relations**`ANTHR 2420`
- *Cattle Bring Us to Our Enemies: Turkana Ecology, Politics & Raiding in a Disequilibrium System* by J. Terrence McCabe
- *Dispossessing the Wilderness: Indian Removal & the Making of the National Parks* by Mark David Spence
 
**Culture & Mental Health: Anthropological Perspectives** `ANTHR 2424`
- *The Occupied Clinic: Militarism & Care in Kashmir* by Saiba Varma
- *Psychiatric Encounters: Madness & Modernity in Yucatan, Mexico* by Beatriz M. Reyes-Foster
- *Our Most Troubling Madness: Case Studies in Schizophrenia across Cultures* by Luhrmann & Marrow
- *A Disability of the Soul: An Ethnography of Schizophrenia & Mental Illness in Contemporary Japan* by Karen Nakamura
- *The Pastoral Clinic: Addiction & Dispossession along the Rio Grande* by Angela Garcia
- *Mental Disorder: Anthropological Insights* by Nichola Khan 

**The Rise & Fall of Civilizations** `ANTHR 2430`
- *Ancient Civilizations* by Scarre & Fagan
 
**South Asian Religions in Practice: The Healing Traditions** `ANTHR 2546`
- *The Indian Way: An Introduction to the Philosophies & Religions of India* by John M Koller
- *Shamans, Mystics & Doctors: A Psychological Inquiry into India & its Healing Traditions* by Sudhir Kakar
- *Medusa's Hair: An Essay on Personal Symbols & Religious Experience* by Gananath Obeyesekere
- *Darsan: Seeing the Divine Image in India* by Diana Eck
- *Given to the Goddess* by Lucinda Ramberg
- *In Amma's Healing Room: Gender & Vernacular Islam in South India* by Joyce Burkhalter Flueckiger
- *Gabriel of Madness: Islamic Poetry & Ethics in an Age of Hindu Nationalism* by Anand Vivek Taneja
- *Mudpacks and Prozac: Experiencing Ayurvedic, Biomedical & Religious Healing* by Murphy Halliburton

## Senior level

**Time & History in Ancient Mexico** `ANTHR 4256`
- *The Codex Borgia: A Full-Color Restoration of the Ancient Mexican Manuscript* by Díaz & Rodgers
- *Portraying the Aztec Past: The Codices Boturini, Azcatitlan & Aubin* by Angela Herren Rajagopalan
- *Deciphering Aztec Hieroglyphs: A Guide to Nahuatl Writing* by Gordon Whittaker |
