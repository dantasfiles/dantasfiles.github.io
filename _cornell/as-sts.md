## Sophomore level

**Ten Technologies That Shook the World?** `STS 2381`
- *Trinity: A Graphic History of the First Atomic Bomb* by Jonathan Fetter-Vorm
- *Technology Matters: Questions to Live With* by David E. Nye

**Nature-Culture: Ethnographic Approaches to Human Environment Relations** `BSOC 2420`
- *Cattle Bring Us to Our Enemies: Turkana Ecology, Politics & Raiding in a Disequilibrium System* by J. Terrence McCabe
- *Dispossessing the Wilderness: Indian Removal & the Making of the National Parks* by Mark David Spence

**Medicine & Healing in China** `BSOC/STS 2561`
- *Chinese Medicine & Healing* by Hinrichs & Barnes
- *Early Chinese Medical Literature: The Mawangdui Medical Manuscripts* by Donald Harper

## Junior level

**Environmental Governance** `BSOC/STS 3311`
- *The Politics of the Earth* by John S. Dryzek
