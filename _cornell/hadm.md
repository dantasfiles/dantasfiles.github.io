**Financial Accounting** `HADM 1210 (fa26)` / **Financial Accounting Principles** `HADM 2230 (fa26)`

**Principles of Food & Beverage Operations Management** `HADM 1361 (fa26)`
- *The Book of Yields: Accuracy in Food Costing & Purchasing* by Francis T. Lynch

**Managerial Accounting** `HADM 2210 (fa26)`

**Finance** `HADM 2220 (fa26)`
- *Fundamentals of Corporate Finance* by Ross et al.

**Principles of Hospitality Real Estate** `HADM 2221 (fa26)`
- *Real Estate Finance & Investments: Risks and Opportunities* by Linneman & Kirsch
