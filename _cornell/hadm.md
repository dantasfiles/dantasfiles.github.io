## Freshman level

**Financial Accounting** `HADM 1210` / **Financial Accounting Principles** `HADM 2230`
- *Financial Accounting* by Libby et al.

**Principles of Food & Beverage Operations Management** `HADM 1361`
- *The Book of Yields: Accuracy in Food Costing & Purchasing* by Francis T. Lynch 

## Sophomore level

**Managerial Accounting** `HADM 2210`
- *Managerial Accounting for Managers* by Noreen et al.

**Finance** `HADM 2220`
- *Fundamentals of Corporate Finance* by Ross et al.

**Principles of Hospitality Real Estate** `HADM 2221`
- *Real Estate Finance & Investments: Risks and Opportunities* by Linneman & Kirsch
